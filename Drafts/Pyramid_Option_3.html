The Madlib. One sentence about her, blanks visible from the start with their labels underneath — the classic form. She taps a blank, picks a word, it lands in the sentence, and Found says one line about it before the next blank opens. Any filled blank can be tapped again to change it.

Every blank offers both poles. A Madlib that only offers symptoms can’t record "this part of me is fine" — and the Result’s "later, and that’s good news" is only honest because we asked. The priority clause joins the sentence only when weight and hormones tie.

Deliberate deviation: age opens the sentence instead of coming last, and the three blanks are visible before the Result. This option trades two locked rules for the form’s own logic — that’s the comparison it exists to make.
