# Result screen content — the 10 endings

What's shown on the Result screen, matching the structure in the drafts: an eyebrow,
a headline, a oneliner, and 3 rows (one per pyramid layer) — each with a status, a
name, a subtitle, and a short "why" that expands.

## Fixed on every Result (never changes)

- **Eyebrow:** *Your starting point*
- **The 3 layers**, always shown in this order, names/subtitles never change:

| Layer | Name | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | Weight & metabolism | Weight care, with GLP-1s |
| Hormones | Hormones | Hormone care, with HRT |
| Peptides | Longevity & peptides | Fine-tuning, with peptides |

Only two things ever change per person: **which status each layer gets**, and the
**headline + oneliner** at the top.

## The 4 statuses a layer can have

Every layer lands in one of these — this is the only "why" text that's ever shown,
reused across all 10 endings:

| Status | When it applies | Why-text shown |
|---|---|---|
| **Start here** | This is her lead layer | *(Weight)* "This is where your numbers and how you feel line up — the layer the others rest on, so moving it tends to move them too." <br> *(Hormones)* "Your base came back steady, so this is where your signals point next." <br> *(Peptides)* "Your base and middle layer are steady, so this is genuinely yours to start on." |
| **Worth watching** | Above the lead, and it's a yes | *(Hormones)* "Worth keeping an eye on — not urgent while the base settles. Some of it may ease once it does." <br> *(Peptides)* "You told us you're curious about staying ahead — worth building on once the base is steady." |
| **Later, and that's good news** | Above the lead, and it's a no | *(Hormones)* "Steady for now. It's not a rejection — there's something more useful to do first." <br> *(Peptides)* "Peptides work best once the base is steady. Being at the top isn't a rejection — there's something more useful to do first." |
| **Steady** | Before the lead (always a no, already resolved) | *(Weight)* "Your numbers came back steady. Nothing here is asking to go first." <br> *(Hormones)* "Steady — nothing here is asking for attention." <br> *(Peptides)* "Nothing here is asking to go first either." |
