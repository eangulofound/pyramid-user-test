# The question script — one story, every path

Same questions and reacts for all 3 options — only how she answers changes (typed/voice ·
chips · blanks). What differs turn to turn is only the **order**, based on what she says
first. Below is every question asked, in order, for every possible starting answer.

**What each answer is quietly deciding (the pyramid, in plain terms):**
- **Weight care** (bottom of the pyramid) — decided by height + weight
- **Hormone care** (middle) — opened by age: at 40 or older, a follow-up question
  about signs decides it (yes/no). Under 40 it stays no, with no extra question.
- **Peptides / prevention** (top) — one question, asked to everyone, no matter what

None of this — the pyramid, the layer names, the yes/no — is ever shown to her until
the Result at the end.

---

## Question 1 — always first (she can pick more than one)

> **How have you been feeling lately?**

| She picks (alone) | React | Then go to |
|---|---|---|
| Heavier than I want to be | *That's the one most people lead with.* | **Path 1** |
| Not sleeping well | *Those nights drag a lot with them.* | **Path 2** |
| Foggy — I can't think straight | *Hard to describe, easy to dismiss. Noted.* | **Path 2** |
| Tired, all the time | *That kind of tired has more than one cause — let's find yours.* | **Path 3** |
| Off, but I can't pin it down | *That's the one most people find hardest to put into words.* | **Path 4** |

If she picks 2 or more → React: *"That's a lot to carry at once — and it rarely arrives
one at a time."* Only her **first** pick decides which path she's on.

## Path 1 — started with "Heavier than I want to be"

**Q2. How tall are you?** *(Roughly is fine.)*
→ React: *"Noted."*

**Q3. And roughly what do you weigh?**
→ React: *"Thank you — being straight with numbers gets you a straight answer back."*
*(This is where Weight care is decided: yes or no. Never shown to her.)*

**Q4. Beyond that, is staying ahead — energy, recovery, longevity — something you're
curious about too?**
- "Not really — I'll deal with things if they come" → React: *"Fair enough — no need
  to fix what isn't asking for it."*
- "Yes — I like being proactive about that" → React: *"Good instinct — that's worth
  building on."*
*(Decides Peptides: yes or no.)*

**Q5. And how old are you?**
- Under 40 → React: *"Thanks."* → *(Hormone care = no — the window hasn't
  opened)* → skip **Q5b**
- 40s / 50s / 60+ → React: *"Thanks."* → go to **Q5b**

**Q5b. (only if she's 40 or older) Any signs of a shift? Cycles changing, night
sweats, sudden heat.**
- "Yes — some of that" → React: *"Good instinct to name it out loud."* (Hormone care = yes)
- "No — none of that" → React: *"Steady is a good answer too."* (Hormone care = no)

**Q6. (only if Weight care = yes AND Hormone care = yes) If one thing changed first —
your weight, or your sleep and cycle — which would you want it to be?**
- "My weight" → React: *"Starred — that's your priority."*
- "My sleep and cycle" → React: *"Starred — that's your priority."*

→ **Go to Result**
